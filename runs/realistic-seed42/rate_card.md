# Merchant Rate Card - MERCH-0001

Effective 1 Jul 2026 unless superseded below. All fees exclusive of GST.

## Card MDR

| Card Type | Slab | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Credit | Up to Rs.2,000.00 | 1.80% |
| Credit | Rs.2,000.00 - Rs.10,000.00 | 1.60% |
| Credit | Above Rs.10,000.00 | 1.40% |
| Debit  | Up to Rs.2,000.00 | 0.90% |
| Debit  | Rs.2,000.00 - Rs.10,000.00 | 0.80% |
| Debit  | Above Rs.10,000.00 | 0.70%, capped at Rs.70.00 per transaction |

## UPI / Netbanking / Wallet
UPI: no MDR, flat Rs.2.00/txn processing fee.
Netbanking: 1.75% + Rs.10.00/txn.
Wallet: 1.90% + Rs.3.00/txn.

## International transactions
+2.00% surcharge on gross, card transactions only.

## Taxes
GST @ 18% applies on all fee lines above.

## TDS (Section 194-O)
Not applicable - merchant is not classified as an e-commerce marketplace
operator under the current registration on file. (e-commerce marketplace operator transactions under Section 194-O)
